Dismay

Psychological Horror Experience

Overview

Dismay is a psychological horror game centered around an urban explorer whose investigations into abandoned locations unknowingly bring something home with him.

The experience focuses on subtle dread, environmental storytelling, and escalating paranormal activity rather than constant jump scares.

Gameplay

Players investigate disturbances throughout their home while reviewing recorded footage and uncovering the connection between each haunting.
